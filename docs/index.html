Why one car

Because rushed work is how people get hurt.

A shop runs six cars through a bay at once and every one of them waits on a part, a lift or a tech. I take one. If your brake job turns into a seized caliper bolt, there's nobody behind you in line, and the job still gets done today.

Questions

The ones I get every week.

Do you really only take one car a day?

Yes. That's the whole model. It's why the day doesn't blow up when a bolt snaps, and it's why days book out.

Which days can I book?

Sunday, Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday. Pick one on the form and I'll confirm by text.

What does the $50 diagnostic cover?

Me coming out, hearing the whole story, pulling codes, testing, and giving you a real diagnosis with a price. If the fix is outside what I do, I tell you and the visit is free.

Why do some jobs have to be dropped off?

Anything internal to the engine — timing chains, head gaskets, teardown — can take two to three days and needs a controlled space. Those come to my place. The diagnostic decides the right setup before any repair is booked.

Do I need to buy the parts?

No. I'll quote parts and labor together before anything starts. If you already bought parts, bring them and we'll talk about it.

Where do you go?

Bozeman, Belgrade, Four Corners, Manhattan, Livingston and the rest of the Gallatin Valley. Further out, ask.

What don't you do?

Transmission rebuilds, body work, alignments, tire mounting, and anything that needs a rack. I'll say so on the first call.

How do I pay?

When the work is done, from the invoice I hand you. No deposit to book.
